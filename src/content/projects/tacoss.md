**TACOSS** (Telescope Automation, Control, and Operation Software Suite) is our comprehensive open-source software platform for modern astronomical observatories.

The modular architecture consists of a robust **core** (TIC, IU, drivers, telemetry, NATS) that provides essential telescope control and automation, complemented by powerful **extensions** including data processing pipelines, databases, AI assistants, and advanced telemetry visualization with Grafana.
